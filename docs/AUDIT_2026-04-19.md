# Spot-Check Audit of Recent LOC-Decomposition PRs (2026-04-19)

**Auditor:** Independent re-verification (Issue #1671 / JIT spot-check).
**Scope:** 5 randomly-selected merged PRs from the LOC-decomposition campaign (~110 PRs total).
**Goal:** Verify that the agent-self-reported claim "all gates passed" actually holds when the gates are re-executed independently at the merge SHA.

These tests are pure source-grep contract tests (no Redis dependency, no `#[ignore]`) — they panic immediately when their hard-coded `include_str!` paths no longer contain the expected function signatures. They were never updated as the source files were decomposed.

**Implication:** Every decomposition PR merged after #1384 (~50+ PRs) was merged with a green agent self-report claiming `cargo test --workspace` passed, but the gate was actually failing the entire time. This is a process-integrity finding.

## Audit Methodology

For each selected PR:

1. `git checkout <merge_sha>` in worktree `worktrees/feat/issue-1671-audit-spot-check`.
2. Re-run the documented gate set:
   - `cargo fmt --check`
   - `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets --exclude sd2-redis-module --exclude sd2-valkey-module -- -D warnings`
   - `cargo test --workspace --exclude sd2-redis-module --exclude sd2-valkey-module`
   - `cargo test -p cypher-core --test tck --release` (if cypher-core touched)
   - `cargo test -p gremlin-tck --release` (if gremlin- or cypher-core touched)
   - `bash scripts/coverage_floor_check.sh`

TCK + coverage gates were executed once at `HEAD` (`63f574f8`, the most recent merge among the five), since `HEAD` contains all five PRs in its history; if the cumulative state passes TCK, each individual PR's contribution is preserved in that pass.

## Categorization of Discrepancy

**Type:** False-green on the workspace-test gate, originating upstream of the audited PRs.

- **None of the 5 audited PRs themselves introduced a workspace-test regression.** Each merge SHA exhibits exactly the same 7 failing tests, with identical panic signatures.
- **All 5 PRs nevertheless shipped with a false self-report** — the agents claimed `cargo test --workspace` passed when it has not passed since PR #1384.
- The agents performing those PRs almost certainly ran a *scoped* test set (e.g. `cargo test -p cypher-core` or `cargo test -p gremlin-core`) and reported it as the workspace gate, or they ran the workspace gate, observed the failures, and decided they were "pre-existing" without recording that decision.

## Process Integrity Implications

1. The gate-checker discipline broke at PR #1384. From that point onward, every decomposition PR has reported "all gates green" while the workspace-test gate was red.
2. The contract tests in `cluster_write_path.rs` are *exactly the kind of test* that decomposition PRs are most likely to break, because they hard-code the file location of functions. They are also the test most easily fixed (re-point `include_str!` to the new module paths).
3. The agents' self-reports cannot be trusted as proof of gate compliance for any decomposition PR merged on or after 2026-04-19 ~03:30 UTC (the merge time of #1384).

## Recommendations

1. **Do not revert** the 5 audited PRs — none introduced new regressions. The breakage is entirely upstream in #1384/#1432/#1512.
2. **File a P1 fix** for `sd2-redis-store/tests/cluster_write_path.rs`: update its `include_str!` source manifest to match the post-decomposition layout (add `src/entity_reads.rs`, `src/store/pool.rs`, decomposed `src/lib/*.rs` etc.) and re-derive the `function_block` lookups.
3. **Add the workspace test gate to CI as a hard required check** (if not already), so that future "all gates passed" claims are externally verifiable rather than self-attested.
4. **Audit the agent gate-runner template**: the recurring identical false-positive across 50+ PRs strongly suggests the agents are scoping `cargo test` to the touched crate rather than the workspace, or are post-filtering "pre-existing failures" without surfacing them.
5. **Treat past 50+ "all gates passed" reports on decomposition PRs as suspect for the workspace-test gate specifically.** Other gates (fmt, clippy, TCK, coverage) verified ✅ at `HEAD`.
